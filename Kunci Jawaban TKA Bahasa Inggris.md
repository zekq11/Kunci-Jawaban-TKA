## A. Reading Comprehension

### Text 1 (untuk soal nomor 1 sampai 3)

Reading every day is one of the simplest habits a student can build. Even fifteen minutes before bed can improve vocabulary, concentration, and writing skills. Researchers also say that people who read regularly tend to feel less stressed, because reading gives the mind a break from screens and noise. To make the habit last, students should choose books they truly enjoy and read at the same time each day.

1\. What is the main idea of the text?

- A. Students must buy many books.
- B. Reading daily gives students several benefits.
- C. Screens are harmful to all people.
- D. Writing is harder than reading.
- E. Bedtime is the only time to read.

Jawaban: B

**Pembahasan:** Seluruh paragraf membahas manfaat membaca setiap hari, yaitu kosakata, konsentrasi, menulis, dan berkurangnya stres. Itulah gagasan utamanya.

2\. According to the text, how can students make the reading habit last?

- A. By reading only difficult books
- B. By reading for several hours on weekends
- C. By choosing enjoyable books and reading at a fixed time
- D. By reading with friends every night
- E. By avoiding any kind of noise

Jawaban: C

**Pembahasan:** Kalimat terakhir menyebut dua cara: memilih buku yang disukai dan membaca pada jam yang sama setiap hari.

3\. The word "*concentration*" in the text is closest in meaning to ...

- A. attention
- B. memory
- C. imagination
- D. confidence
- E. creativity

Jawaban: A

**Pembahasan:** Concentration berarti kemampuan memusatkan pikiran, sepadan dengan attention.

## B. Grammar dan Structure

4\. My brother ... football when the rain started to fall.

- A. plays
- B. is playing
- C. was playing
- D. has played
- E. will play

Jawaban: C

**Pembahasan:** Ada dua kejadian lampau: kegiatan yang sedang berlangsung (past continuous) dan kejadian yang memotongnya (simple past, "started"). Maka pola yang tepat adalah *was playing*.

5\. If I ... more time yesterday, I would have finished the project.

- A. have
- B. had
- C. would have
- D. had had
- E. having

Jawaban: D

**Pembahasan:** Ini conditional type 3 (pengandaian yang tidak terjadi di masa lalu). Rumusnya: If + past perfect, would have + V3. Jadi *had had*.

6\. The new library, ... was built last year, is open to the public.

- A. who
- B. whom
- C. whose
- D. which
- E. what

Jawaban: D

**Pembahasan:** "The new library" adalah benda, sehingga relative pronoun yang dipakai adalah *which*.

7\. Neither the teacher nor the students ... aware of the schedule change.

- A. is
- B. was
- C. has been
- D. were
- E. be

Jawaban: D

**Pembahasan:** Pada pola *neither ... nor*, kata kerja mengikuti subjek yang paling dekat. Subjek terdekat adalah "the students" (jamak), maka dipakai *were*.

8\. The report ... by the committee before the meeting begins.

- A. will review
- B. will be reviewed
- C. reviewed
- D. is reviewing
- E. has reviewing

Jawaban: B

**Pembahasan:** Laporan adalah objek yang dikenai tindakan, dan ada kata "by the committee". Kalimat pasif masa depan: will be + V3.

9\. She is interested ... learning about marine biology.

- A. on
- B. at
- C. with
- D. in
- E. for

Jawaban: D

**Pembahasan:** Kolokasi yang benar adalah *interested in*.

## C. Vocabulary

10\. The scientist gave a ... explanation, so everyone understood the experiment easily.

- A. vague
- B. clear
- C. complicated
- D. hidden
- E. careless

Jawaban: B

**Pembahasan:** Akibatnya "everyone understood easily", jadi penjelasannya harus *clear* (jelas).

11\. The synonym of "*reluctant*" is ...

- A. eager
- B. unwilling
- C. generous
- D. certain
- E. brave

Jawaban: B

**Pembahasan:** Reluctant berarti enggan, sepadan dengan unwilling. Lawan katanya adalah eager.

12\. The antonym of "*scarce*" is ...

- A. rare
- B. limited
- C. abundant
- D. valuable
- E. fragile

Jawaban: C

**Pembahasan:** Scarce berarti langka atau sedikit. Lawan katanya adalah abundant (melimpah).

13\. Please ... your phone before entering the examination room.

- A. turn off
- B. turn up
- C. turn into
- D. turn down
- E. turn over

Jawaban: A

**Pembahasan:** Phrasal verb *turn off* berarti mematikan, sesuai konteks ruang ujian.

## Tips mengerjakan soal Bahasa Inggris

Baca teks reading lebih dulu secara cepat, lalu garis bawahi kata kunci pada pertanyaan. Untuk grammar, tentukan dulu tenses dan subjeknya sebelum melihat pilihan. Untuk vocabulary, cari petunjuk dari kalimat di sekitar kata yang ditanyakan.

Catatan: kunci jawaban di atas hanya untuk latihan dan belajar mandiri. Isi soal ujian sebenarnya bisa berbeda.
